# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Meridian Technologies! This guide will help you navigate your first days and weeks at the company.

## 1. First Day Checklist
*   **8:30 AM:** Arrive at the office (if working on-site) or log in to your remote workstation.
*   **9:00 AM:** Attend the HR Welcome Orientation via Zoom. You will receive an email with the link.
*   **10:30 AM:** IT Setup. Log in to your laptop using the temporary credentials sent to your personal email. Change your password immediately following the guidelines in the Information Security Policy.
*   **11:00 AM:** Enroll in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via Okta.
*   **12:00 PM:** Team Lunch (virtual or in-person).
*   **1:30 PM:** Meet with your manager for an introductory 1:1.
*   **3:00 PM:** Access Workday and complete your I-9 verification and direct deposit setup.

## 2. First Week Goals
By the end of your first week, you should have completed the following:
* Complete all mandatory security and compliance training modules in the Learning Management System (LMS). This is a strict requirement.
* Gain access to GitHub, Jira, AWS, and Slack. If you are missing any permissions, submit an IT ticket.
* Schedule a 30-minute "coffee chat" with at least three members of your immediate team.
* Review the Acceptable Use Policy and Employee Handbook.
* Push your first small code change or documentation update (for engineering/product roles).

## 3. First Month Expectations
*   Complete role-specific training as assigned by your manager.
*   Present a small topic or "lightning talk" in your team's weekly meeting.
*   Establish your initial 90-day goals with your manager.

## 4. Mentor Program
Meridian pairs every new hire with a "Meridian Buddy" – an experienced employee from outside your direct reporting chain.
* Your buddy will reach out to you on your first day.
* You are encouraged to meet with your buddy weekly for the first month, and bi-weekly for the next two months.
* Your buddy can help answer questions about company culture, navigate internal processes, and provide general support.
