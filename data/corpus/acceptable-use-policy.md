# IT Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy outlines the acceptable use of computer equipment at Meridian Technologies Inc. 

## 1. Company Devices
Meridian provides equipment (laptops, monitors, mobile phones) to facilitate business operations. 
* Company devices are for business purposes. Occasional personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work productivity or violate any company policies.
* Employees are responsible for the physical security of their issued devices. Laptops must not be left visible in unattended vehicles.
* Any loss or theft of a company device must be reported to IT and InfoSec within 24 hours.
* Modifying the operating system, disabling antivirus software, or attempting to bypass endpoint management controls is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Personal Devices (BYOD)
Employees may use personal smartphones or tablets to access company email and calendar applications.
* To access company resources on a personal device, employees must install the Mobile Device Management (MDM) profile provided by IT.
* The MDM profile allows IT to enforce security policies (e.g., device passcode lock) and remotely wipe company data if the device is lost or the employee leaves Meridian.
* Accessing source code, customer databases, or Restricted data from personal devices is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Prohibited Software
Employees may not install unauthorized software on company devices.
* Software required for business purposes must be requested through the IT Service Desk and approved by a manager.
* Peer-to-peer (P2P) file-sharing applications (e.g., BitTorrent), unauthorized VPNs, and cryptocurrency mining software are expressly prohibited on the corporate network and company devices.

## 4. Monitoring Disclosure
Meridian reserves the right to monitor, log, and inspect all communications, files, and internet traffic traversing its networks and systems.
* Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-provided devices, networks, or accounts.
* Routine monitoring is conducted to ensure system performance, enforce security policies, and investigate potential policy violations.

## 5. Social Media Guidelines
When using social media, whether personally or professionally, employees must not disclose Confidential or Restricted company information.
* Do not speak on behalf of Meridian unless explicitly authorized by the PR department.
* Be respectful and avoid discriminatory, harassing, or defamatory language that could reflect poorly on the company.
