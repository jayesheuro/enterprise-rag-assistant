# Data Retention Policy

This policy dictates how long Meridian Technologies Inc. must retain various types of data and the procedures for data disposal.

## 1. Retention Schedules

Different categories of data have distinct retention periods dictated by business needs and legal requirements.

* **Customer Data:** Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and usage data of our customers must be retained for the duration of their active contract plus **7 years** after contract termination for audit purposes.
* **Financial Records:** Invoices, tax documents, bank statements, and payroll records must be kept for **10 years**.
* **Employee Records:** Personnel files, performance reviews, and disciplinary records must be retained for **5 years** after the employee's departure.
* **System Logs:** Application, network, and security logs must be retained for a minimum of **90 days** in hot storage and an additional **365 days** in cold storage (e.g., AWS Glacier).
* **Internal Communications:** Slack messages and internal emails are retained for **2 years**. After 2 years, they are automatically purged unless under a legal hold.
* **Contracts and Legal Agreements:** Must be retained indefinitely during the active term and for **15 years** after expiration or termination.

## 2. Deletion Procedures
When data reaches the end of its retention period, it must be securely and permanently deleted.
* **Digital Data:** Must be securely erased using industry-standard wiping tools (e.g., NIST 800-88 guidelines). Simply deleting the file or formatting the drive is insufficient for Confidential or Restricted data.
* **Physical Media:** Paper documents containing Confidential or Restricted information must be cross-cut shredded. Hard drives, USBs, and other physical storage media must be physically destroyed by the IT department.

## 3. Legal Holds
In the event of impending litigation, investigation, or audit, the Legal department will issue a "Legal Hold."
* A Legal Hold suspends all standard data destruction policies for the specified data.
* Employees notified of a Legal Hold must preserve all relevant documents, emails, and data until the hold is officially lifted by the Legal department in writing.
* Failure to comply with a Legal Hold may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, as well as potential legal liability.
