# Meridian Technologies Inc. Employee Handbook

Welcome to Meridian Technologies Inc. (Meridian). This handbook outlines our policies, procedures, and benefits.

## 1. Work Hours and Location
Core business hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in your local time zone. Employees are expected to be available for meetings during these hours. A standard workweek consists of 40 hours.

Meridian supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Office attendance is mandatory on Tuesdays and Thursdays for all engineering and product teams. Remote work locations must have a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps down and 10 Mbps up.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Meridian offers a flexible PTO policy designed to support work-life balance.
- **0-5 years tenure:** 20 days of PTO per calendar year.
- **5+ years tenure:** 25 days of PTO per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave:** 10 days per year, regardless of tenure.
- **Volunteer Days:** 2 days per year for approved charitable activities.

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the next calendar year. Rollover days expire on March 31st of the following year.

## 3. Holidays
Meridian observes 11 standard company holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

## 4. Dress Code
Meridian maintains a "Dress for Your Day" policy. Casual attire (jeans, t-shirts, sneakers) is acceptable for typical days in the office. If meeting with clients or external partners, business casual or business formal attire is required. Please ensure all clothing is clean, neat, and professional. Avoid clothing with offensive language or graphics.

## 5. Benefits Overview
**Health Insurance:** Meridian covers 85% of premiums for employees and 70% for dependents. We offer PPO and HDHP plans through Blue Cross.
**401(k) Match:** Meridian matches 100% of the first 4% of employee contributions, and 50% of the next 2%. Contributions vest immediately.
**Wellness Stipend:** Employees receive a $100 monthly stipend for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or wellness apps.
**Commuter Benefits:** Pre-tax transit accounts are available. Employees commuting to the office more than 10 days a month are eligible for a $50 monthly transit subsidy.

For IT and security policies, please refer to the Information Security Policy.
